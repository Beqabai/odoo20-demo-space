BPMN Process Management

Process repository, BPMN 2.0 modeler / viewer and knowledge hub

Publish the official map of how your company works, directly in Odoo. Employees open a process, click a task and read the work instruction, see the responsible department and jump to the right Odoo screen. Process managers model, import, review, version and publish.

  • Full BPMN 2.0 modeler and read-only viewer (bpmn-js).
  • Import from draw.io (.drawio, .xml, .svg, .png), Microsoft Visio (.vsdx) and any BPMN 2.0 tool (Camunda, Signavio, bpmn.io, Bizagi).
  • Export to BPMN 2.0, draw.io, SVG and PNG.
  • Soft links: lanes to departments, job positions or user groups; tasks to Odoo menus, records (Knowledge, Documents when installed) and URLs.
  • Imported diagrams are flagged for review; lanes are matched to departments by name.
  • Draft, review, publish and versioning with chatter history.
  • No impact on sales, purchase, inventory or accounting documents.
  • Odoo 19 and 20, Community and Enterprise. Georgian translation included.
